Audit updated: 2026-10-17 (UTC); original baseline: 2026-10-15 (PDT). Baseline: the full 663-line [creator prompt](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1EHo5qNubl2eso078n1XwmvPHNETfsc84HMN9j9sw7Sg/edit?usp=sharing). Its SHA-256 is `6201b66564cc40e20f28baaba7726c226baf638733be97eab1e50f4c52133487`.

The original studio interface, server adapters and local demonstration workflows are implemented. The creator prompt's complete production definition of done is **not yet met**: real provider execution, account integrations, paid billing acceptance and visual parity remain unverified. The published release is a **read-only showcase** at [fusion-gen-matrix.vercel.app](https://fusion-gen-matrix.vercel.app). GitHub publication or a Vercel READY status does not establish live generation support.

Statuses: **Implemented** means present in source; **Tested** names the verified environment; **Configuration required** means implementation exists but external setup/acceptance is outstanding; **Missing** means the requested behavior or proof is absent. A row may have more than one status.

## Exact-clone and production acceptance gaps

This is an **original rebranded implementation**, not a screenshot-perfect or template-derived clone. The visible differences include original typography/artwork and justified row packing, original icons, limited preset content and no copied Quanta components. The public Explore/image/video layouts were captured in this pass; private source-site workflows were not accessed. Every-route desktop/mobile side-by-side parity is unproven.

The production acceptance sequence is: configure MongoDB/Auth.js/Resend/R2/Higgsfield; verify signed-upload origins and browser CORS; verify one bounded real image and video workflow; schedule and exercise reconciliation; configure approved Stripe test prices and verify subscription/top-up/Portal/receipts; then approve legal content and paid launch. A real generation per documented family, original provider-generated hero/preset media, Lighthouse and deployed E2E remain outstanding.

The final callback latency issue found in this audit was fixed and covered by focused tests. No further concrete blocker to a read-only showcase was established. The public browser and mutation-boundary checks in the [launch runbook](launch-runbook.md) passed on 2026-10-17; live-service acceptance remains separate.

Supercomputer agents, Canvas, Cinema multi-shot timelines, training, 3D, teams, Academy, contests, plugins and customer APIs remain outside the creator prompt's v1 scope.
